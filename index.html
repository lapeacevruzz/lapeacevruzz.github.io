♞ VR Chess

In VR: aim at a piece and hold the trigger to pick it up, move it over a square and release to place it. Press Y (or X) on the left controller for the settings menu.

On desktop: click to play, M opens the menu.

4 Player Chess: menu ▸ Crazy Chess ▸ turn it ON, then New Game.

Secret Queen: menu ▸ Crazy Chess ▸ Secret Queen ON, then New Game and tap one of your pawns. It moves like a queen; your opponent can't see which one until it first does.

Account: sign in here, or inside VR from menu ▸ Online ▸ Sign in. You stay signed in on this device. Online wins, losses and draws are saved to your account.
