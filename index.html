♞ VR Chess

Passthrough: 'Enter Passthrough' shows your real room and puts the board on your real table or floor. Touch the table where the board's centre goes and pinch (or pull the trigger), then touch the far edge (the opponent's side) and pinch again. Menu ▸ Place Board redoes it. Not available together with Synced Environment.

In VR: aim at a piece and hold the trigger to pick it up, move it over a square and release to place it. Press Y (or X) on the left controller for the settings menu.

Press A (right controller) opens chat, B (right controller) for a 2D analysis map: try moves for either side, then close it to snap back to the real board.

On desktop: click to play, M opens the menu, B the map.

Resign / Draw: open the map (B); the strip on its right has Resign (asks you to confirm) and, in online 1v1 games against a person, Offer Draw (your opponent gets a pop-up to accept or decline). The strip on the map's left lists every move; when a game ends, Stockfish reviews it and shows how many Brilliant, Great, Best… Blunder moves each player made.

4 Player Chess: menu ▸ Crazy Chess ▸ turn it ON, then New Game.

Big Chess: menu ▸ Crazy Chess ▸ Big Chess ON, then New Game. Turn it on together with 4 Player Chess for a life-size 4 player board. A life-size board (pieces about 1.7 m tall) in its own arena, the same for everyone in an online game. In VR: point at your piece, hold the trigger to pick it up, walk with the left thumbstick (right stick turns) to drag it onto a square, then let go. On desktop: click to select and move, WASD to walk, Q/E to turn.

Account: sign in here, or inside VR from menu ▸ Online ▸ Sign in. You stay signed in on this device. Online wins, losses and draws are saved to your account. Ranked 1v1 games between two signed-in players also change your Elo rating (everyone starts at 1200).
